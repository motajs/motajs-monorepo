<objective>
Declare the contracts core depends on, and make the package's public surface both complete and honestly recorded.

Purpose: PORT-01 is not "write four interfaces" — it is "write the four interfaces the known consumers actually need, and nothing speculative". `FsPort` is grounded in the resource layer's real Phase-4 call sites (seven operations, listed in the research), `HostPort` in the host's endpoint shape, and `EngineAdapter` / `PreviewAdapter` are deliberately thin because Phase 5 and Phase 11 own their growth (D-14). </objective>
