  <action>
    Extend the registry closure in `packages/libs/editor-core/lib/kernel/core.ts` — no new exports, no change to the six-member instance surface.

    Kind validation: an entry whose kind does not match the module-private `KIND_PATTERN` is rejected with a single diagnostic whose `code` is `DIAGNOSTIC_CODES.capabilityKindInvalid` and whose `target` is the composed `kind:id`; the returned `disposer` is a no-op function. The pattern accepts `command`, `keybinding`, `code.language`, `table.fieldEditor`, `acme.anything` and `acme.my-kind`, and rejects the empty string, a leading digit, a leading or trailing dot, an empty segment, and any whitespace. `id` has no format rule; it is stored as given, including hostile-looking values such as `__proto__` and `constructor`.

    Duplicate rejection: when an entry already occupies the composed key and its `replaceable` is not true, reject with a single diagnostic whose `code` is `DIAGNOSTIC_CODES.capabilityDuplicate`, whose `owner` is the **existing** entry's owner (so the diagnostic says who occupies the key), and whose `target` is the composed `kind:id`. Severity is `error`. The rejection must leave the map, the stored entry and the teardown stack byte-identical to their pre-call state — assert this from the test, not from prose.
